About the show

A satire about internet content and the people who make it.

SnakeMagic.com is a scripted, indie-style comedy series. Hell-bent on creating the next big viral video, Luke, Levi and the unpredictable SnakeMagic.com production crew set out on a misguided odyssey towards internet fame.

Each episode the gang works hard to produce a video, but running alongside the train wreck are the characters that are driving the show. It is their idiosyncrasies and dysfunctional relationships that shine a light on the absurdist nature of the human condition at the heart of the show's humor.
